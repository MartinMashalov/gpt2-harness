What happens to a training job at each wall:
  memory.max  hard. The cgroup OOM killer picks a process in this cgroup and
              SIGKILLs it. No Python exception, no traceback: the rank just
              disappears and the launcher sees exit code -9. Check
              memory.events' oom_kill counter to tell this apart from a
              hardware or network failure.
  memory.high soft. No kill; the cgroup is throttled into reclaim and the
              step time inflates. This is a throughput bug, not a crash.
  swap        memory.swap.max is 0 here, so there is no paging escape hatch:
              the job hits memory.max and dies rather than going slow. For a
              collective-synchronised job that is the better failure -- one
              rank paging to disk stalls every other rank at the next
              all-reduce, and a hang is harder to diagnose than a kill.
  cpu.max     quota, not a kill. Throttling shows up in cpu.stat and looks
              like a slow dataloader, which looks like a slow GPU.
